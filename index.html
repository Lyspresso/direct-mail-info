Direct Mail Assistant (Personal)

A private email helper for its owner’s Gmail accounts, running locally on their Mac.

The helper searches and reads email, downloads selected attachments, prepares local drafts, and sends messages when its owner instructs it to. Each account is connected through Google’s sign-in and permission screen.

Google permissions are used to identify the connected account, read its mail, and send mail from that account. Credentials are kept in macOS Keychain. This website provides app information only; it has no mailbox access, sign-in form, or message storage.

This is a personal-use application, not a service offered to the public.
