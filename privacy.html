Privacy

This notice describes the local Direct Mail Assistant (Personal) application.

Information accessed

With the owner’s permission, the helper accesses the connected Google email address, mailbox search results, message headers and bodies, and selected attachments. It sends messages only when instructed by the owner.

Use and storage

Google access is used to carry out the owner’s email requests. Authorization credentials are stored in macOS Keychain. Prepared messages are encrypted locally; operation records and account identifiers are stored locally. Attachments are saved only to an explicitly selected path.

AI processing

Email content retrieved for an AI-assisted request may be processed by the AI service the owner uses. That service’s account settings and privacy terms also apply. The local helper does not operate a model-training service or sell email data. Google Workspace data must not be used to train generalized AI models.

Control and removal

The owner can remove a local account connection and revoke the application’s permission in Google Account settings. Downloaded attachments and local records remain until separately removed. This informational website does not collect email content or authorization credentials.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
